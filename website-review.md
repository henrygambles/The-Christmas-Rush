### Design Characteristics:
- **Color Palette**: Racing red (#E63946), Alpine blue (#1D3557), Gold (#FFB703), Snow white (#F8F9FA)
- **Typography**: Bebas Neue for headings (uppercase, bold), Roboto for body text
- **Layout**: Diagonal sections with ski slope aesthetic
- **Animations**: Speed lines, diagonal slide-ins, hover effects
- **Responsive**: Works on mobile and desktop
